Key Features

Everything your workforce needs. Connected.

Manage attendance, field teams, leave, shifts, payroll and operational insights in one platform.

Face-Verified Attendance

Selfie-based punch-in/out with on-device face recognition, reducing buddy punching without dedicated biometric hardware.

Live Field-Force Tracking

Live map location for on-duty field employees, route history, distance and tracking-quality insights.

Leave Management

Configurable leave types, automatic accruals, financial-year balances, holiday calendars and multi-level approvals.

Shifts & Weekly Offs

Day/night and cross-midnight shifts, grace periods, late rules and flexible weekly-off patterns.

Payroll & Compensation

Salary structures, overtime, attendance-linked payroll periods, revision history and payroll-ready reports.

Expenses & Advances

Receipt-based reimbursements and salary advances with configurable policies and approval workflows connected to payroll.

Roles & Access Control

Granular permissions for admins, HR, managers and employees, protected super-admin access and audit logging.

Reports & Analytics

Filtered and exportable attendance, leave, overtime, correction, expense and advance reports.

Trust

Security & Privacy by Design

Workforce data is sensitive. Klyper HRMS treats it that way.

Consent-First Tracking

Location tracking runs only for on-duty field employees, with an in-app disclosure and consent before it ever starts.

On-Device Face Matching

Face verification runs on the employee's own device. Raw photos aren't used for anything beyond attendance verification.

Isolated Deployments

Each organization runs on its own isolated instance and database — your data is never mixed with anyone else's.

Encrypted & Audited

HTTPS everywhere, role-based access, session controls, and audit trails on sensitive operations.
