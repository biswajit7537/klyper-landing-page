Key Features

Key Features

Practical CRM and lead management software tools that help your team respond faster, stay organized, and convert more enquiries into bookings.

01

Capture Leads From Everywhere

Bring in enquiries from Facebook ads, Google ads, your website, referrals, walk-ins, or a spreadsheet import—so every opportunity lands in one place.

02

Fair Lead Assignment

Assign leads yourself or let the system share them evenly across your team. Keep private leads with one agent, or share them so the whole team can help.

03

Hot, Warm & Cold Priorities

Mark every enquiry as Hot, Warm, or Cold so your team instantly knows who needs attention first—and who can wait a little longer.

04

Full Activity History

Keep notes, updates, and next steps on every lead in one clear timeline—so any team member can pick up the conversation without asking around.

05

Your Sales Journey, Your Way

Set the stages that match your process—from new enquiry and follow-up to site visit, office visit, and closed deal.

06

Follow-ups That Never Slip

Schedule the next call or visit, and see today’s and tomorrow’s follow-ups in one list—so no promising lead goes cold.

07

Live Dashboards & Reports

See new leads, conversions, overdue follow-ups, and team performance at a glance—then download reports when you need deeper clarity.

08

Property Inventory Matching

Manage projects, units, and availability—then connect each enquiry to the right property so your team always shows the best fit.

09

Built for Sales Teams

Owners get full oversight; agents get a clear personal workspace with My Leads, Shared Leads, and instant alerts when new enquiries arrive.
