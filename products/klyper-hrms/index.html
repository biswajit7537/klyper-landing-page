Flagship Product

Klyper HRMS

One platform for attendance, leave, payroll, and field-force management — with face-verified punches and real-time workforce visibility.

About the Product

Klyper HRMS is a complete workforce management platform built for modern organizations — from office teams to distributed field forces. It combines face-verified attendance, live location tracking, leave and shift management, payroll processing, and expense workflows into a single system, available as a web application for HR and administrators and a mobile app for every employee.

Built for Office & Field Teams

Face-verified attendance punches for on-site staff, plus live location tracking and route history for field employees — all with clear consent and configurable working-hours policies.

End-to-End HR Operations

Onboarding, roles and permissions, shifts and weekly offs, leave balances, overtime, corrections, payroll, reimbursements, and salary advances — managed in one place with multi-level approvals.

Dedicated Deployment, Customized for You

Every organization gets its own dedicated, isolated deployment — customized to your policies and workflows — with encrypted communication, role-based access control, and full audit trails. Employees use one common mobile app across all deployments.

Key Features

Everything you need to manage your workforce, end to end

Face-Verified Attendance

Employees punch in and out with a selfie verified by on-device face recognition — preventing buddy punching without expensive biometric hardware.

Live Field-Force Tracking

Real-time location of on-duty field employees on a live map, with route history, distance travelled, and tracking-quality insights — active only during working hours with employee consent.

Leave Management

Configurable leave types, automatic monthly accruals, financial-year balances, holiday calendars, and multi-level approval workflows.

Shifts & Weekly Offs

Day and night shifts (including cross-midnight), grace periods, late-arrival rules, and flexible weekly-off patterns assigned per employee or in bulk.

Payroll & Compensation

Salary structures, overtime, attendance-linked payroll periods, compensation revision history, and payroll-ready reports — all connected to attendance data.

Expenses & Advances

Expense reimbursement claims with receipt uploads, salary advance requests, configurable policies, and approval chains that flow straight into payroll.

Roles & Access Control

Fine-grained, permission-based access for admins, HR, managers, and employees — with protected super-admin accounts and full audit logging.

Reports & Analytics

Attendance, leave, overtime, correction, expense, and advance reports with filters and exports — giving HR and management real operational insight.

For Every Employee Mobile + Web

A Web Console for HR, a Mobile App for Everyone

HR and administrators get a powerful web console for policies, approvals, payroll, and live tracking. Employees get a fast mobile app for daily punches, leave requests, expense claims, and their own records.

  • Selfie punch with face verification and offline punch support
  • Apply for leave, corrections, overtime, and advances on the go

Security & Privacy by Design

Workforce data is sensitive. Klyper HRMS treats it that way.

Consent-First Tracking

Location tracking runs only for on-duty field employees, with an in-app disclosure and consent before it ever starts.

On-Device Face Matching

Face verification runs on the employee's own device. Raw photos aren't used for anything beyond attendance verification.

Isolated Deployments

Each organization runs on its own isolated instance and database — your data is never mixed with anyone else's.

Encrypted & Audited

HTTPS everywhere, role-based access, session controls, and audit trails on sensitive operations.
