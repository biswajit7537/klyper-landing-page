About the Product

Klyper Inventory Manager

Klyper Inventory Manager is inventory management and stock management software for organisations that move materials across sites. Track purchases, transfers, updates, and site returns; export reports; control access with roles; and optionally enable AI answers or a supplier bills workflow — all under your own branding.

Live dashboard

  • Greeting and date-aware home screen for daily operations

Multi-project & multi-site inventory

Product catalog masters

  • Maintain Products, Brands, Categories, and Units
  • Active / inactive control, search, filter, and pagination

  • Catalog data auto-fills across purchase, transfer, and return screens

Purchase entry

  • Multi-line purchase bills against a project
  • Product, quantity, invoice details, and amount summary
  • Controlled create / edit / delete by permission
  • Purchase activity reflected on dashboard and reports
05

Stock overview & control

  • Filter by project, product, brand, category, unit, and availability
  • Availability views: In stock, Low stock, Out of stock
  • Paginated overview with CSV export
  • Clear visibility of what each project holds
06

Stock transfer

  • Quantity validation against available source stock

  • Transfer history available in reports

Stock update / adjustments

Site return management

Power Features

Optional modules

Operator-enabled · permission-gated add-ons.

Klyper Assist — AI inventory assistant

Operator-enabled · permission-gated

  • Chat with your live inventory in plain language
  • Ask about stock by project/product, low stock, purchases, transfers, site returns, and updates
  • Grounded answers from your database (read-only tools)
  • Optional Excel downloads from chat answers (when report export is allowed)
  • Works with configured OpenAI-compatible models (including local options such as Ollama)
  • Can be turned off globally for a client at any time

Ask: What’s low stock on Project A this month? — and get an answer from your live data.

Bills & accounts payable workflow

Operator-enabled · permission-gated

  • Supplier master with GSTIN, contacts, address, bank details, and status
  • Submit bills with supplier find-or-create, amount, bill number, notes
  • Attach invoice images or PDFs (up to 5 files, size-limited)
  • Mobile-friendly capture: take a photo or pick files from gallery
  • My Bills for submitters to track their own submissions
  • Accounts inbox to mark bills Paid, On Hold, or Rejected
  • Attachment storage on local disk or MinIO / S3-compatible object storage
  • Attachment storage counted toward organisation quota

Capture the invoice on site, submit it for accounts, and close the loop when it’s paid.
